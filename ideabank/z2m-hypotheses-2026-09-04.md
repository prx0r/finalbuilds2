# z2m → finalbuilds2 Hypotheses

## Strongest Umbrella Hypotheses

### H1: Personalization Arbitrage
**Statement:** AI makes 1:1 customization economically viable at volume, and the moat is recipient memory + occasion reminders + creative recipes, not the image generation itself.
**Evidence:** 66/100 top Etsy sellers customize. Moonpig: 101M reminders/year. Etsy: 30% of $10.5B GMS is personalized. AI production cost: $0.01-0.05/item.
**Confidence:** 0.80
**Children:** H1a (gift engines), H1b (decision engines), H1c (visualization)

### H2: Pain-Attached Digital Products
**Statement:** Sell cheap certainty to people already spending £5k-100k on something else. The information is free; the curation/personalization/packaging is what they pay for.
**Evidence:** £39 kitchen audit attached to £40k renovation. $39 golf plan attached to $500 equipment. $39 moving blueprint attached to £20k relocation.
**Confidence:** 0.85
**Children:** H2a (moving), H2b (golf), H2c (renovation), H2d (wedding)

### H3: Voice AI Collapses Commerce Interaction Cost
**Statement:** Voice AI cost collapsed to $0.07-0.11/min vs $15-25/hr human. One person can run 5 voice agents for 100 merchants.
**Evidence:** Market $3.5B (2026) → $35.2B (2033) at 39% CAGR. Retell $0.07-0.31/min. Inworld $0.013/min.
**Confidence:** 0.75
**Children:** H3a (concierge), H3b (warranty), H3c (delivery), H3d (return), H3e (B2B)

### H4: Geographic Arbitrage Requires Localization
**Statement:** Same product, different countries, different competition. But localization ≠ translation. Need language + currency + payment + shipping + trust.
**Evidence:** Espresso bundle: Sweden 78.9, Germany 71.9, Switzerland 68.5. 80% Finnish consumers buy cross-border. Norway 78%.
**Confidence:** 0.70
**Children:** H4a (Nordic), H4b (DACH), H4c (Benelux)

### H5: Free Distribution First
**Statement:** Validate with $0 before spending. Etsy free listings, Google free Shopping, Pinterest organic, YouTube Shorts.
**Evidence:** 2.5% of John Lewis searches from AI agents. Google free listings across Search, Shopping, Images, Lens, YouTube, Maps, Gemini.
**Confidence:** 0.85
**Children:** H5a (Etsy), H5b (Google free), H5c (Pinterest), H5d (YouTube)

### H6: Factory Is The Product
**Statement:** The system that generates/tests/products is more valuable than any single product. Build the machine, not the output.
**Evidence:** finalbuilds2 ideabank: 100+ generated ideas. Hypothesis tracking. Deterministic experiments. Attribution analytics.
**Confidence:** 0.90
**Children:** H6a (ideabank), H6b (hypothesis), H6c (scoring), H6d (experiments)

### H7: AI Gifting Is The Strongest Single Product
**Statement:** AI makes personalized physical goods economically viable. Moat is recipient memory + occasion reminders + creative recipes.
**Evidence:** Moonpig £373M. Etsy 30% personalized. Amazon launched AI custom merch June 2026. Prodigi card £1.10.
**Confidence:** 0.85
**Children:** H7a (ornaments), H7b (puzzles), H7c (photo books), H7d (cards)
